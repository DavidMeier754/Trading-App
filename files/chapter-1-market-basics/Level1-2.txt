Level: 1-2
Chapter: 1 — Market Basics
Path: Scalping (identical across all paths)
Category: New Theory
Subcategory: Market Structure
Learning Goal: User can explain what a stock market is and identify supply/demand as the driver of price changes.
Purpose: Prices moving is the entire reason trading exists — knowing what actually causes a price to move is the single most useful idea in this whole chapter.

Screens:
1. Title: "Where do people actually trade stocks?" Body: "A stock market is a place — mostly digital today — where buyers and sellers meet to trade shares.".
2. Title: "Two sides, always." Body: "Every trade needs a buyer AND a seller agreeing on a price at the same moment.".
3. Title: "What moves the price?" Body: "When more people want to BUY than SELL, the price tends to rise. When more want to sell than buy, it tends to fall.".
4. Multiple choice. Prompt: "A stock has far more people trying to buy it than sell it right now. What will likely happen to its price?"Four answer options: (A) "It will likely rise" [correct], (B) "It will likely fall", (C) "It will stay exactly the same", (D) "It becomes impossible to trade". Reveal note: "More buying pressure than selling pressure typically pushes price up."
5. Scenario multiple choice. Prompt: "A popular company announces layoffs, and suddenly far more people want to sell their shares than buy. What happens to the price?"Four answer options: (A) "It likely falls" [correct], (B) "It likely rises", (C) "The company stops existing", (D) "Nothing changes at all". Reveal note: "More sellers than buyers pushes price down — the same rule, just the other direction."
6. Matching exercise. Prompt: "Match each term to its meaning." Terms: "Buyer", "Seller", "Market"; Definitions: "Wants to acquire a stock", "Wants to give up a stock", "Where trades happen".
7. True/False. Prompt: "A market is only a physical building."Two answer options "True" / "False" (correct). Reveal note: "Most trading today happens electronically, not in a physical space."
8. Numeric/scenario multiple choice. Prompt: "A stock has 200 buy orders waiting and only 40 sell orders waiting at the current price. What's the more likely next move?"Four answer options: (A) "Price rises" [correct], (B) "Price falls", (C) "Price is unaffected by order counts", (D) "The stock is delisted". Reveal note: "Far more buyers than sellers waiting is a strong signal of rising demand."

All other relevant info: Source — Aziz, Ch. 1; Murphy, Introduction. Prerequisite: 1-1.
