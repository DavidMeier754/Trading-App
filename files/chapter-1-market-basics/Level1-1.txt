Level: 1-1
Chapter: 1 — Market Basics
Path: Scalping (identical across all paths)
Category: New Theory
Subcategory: Market Structure
Learning Goal: User can explain what a stock represents (partial ownership of a company).
Purpose: Every trading decision in this app involves stocks — understanding what you actually own when you buy one is the foundation everything else builds on.

Screens:
1. "Before we trade, let's understand what we're actually trading."
2. Title: "What is a stock?" Body text: "A stock is a small piece of ownership in a company. When you buy one, you own a tiny slice of that business.".
3. Text: "Imagine a company is cut into 100 equal slices. If you own 1 slice, you own 1% of that company.".
4. Prompt: "What does it mean if you own a share of a company?" Four answer options: (A) "You own a small piece of that company" [correct], (B) "You lent the company money that must be repaid", (C) "You get a discount on the company's products", (D) "You become an employee of the company".
5. True/False question. Prompt: "A person who owns shares in a company is called a shareholder." Two answer options: "True" (correct) / "False".
6. Prompt: "A company wants to raise money to build a new factory. Which of these is a way it could do that?" Four answer options: (A) "Sell shares of the company to investors" [correct], (B) "Ask its employees to work unpaid", (C) "Lower its product prices", (D) "Change its company name". Explanation: "Selling shares brings in money from investors who now own part of the company."
7. Fill-in-the-blank. Prompt: "A company sells shares mainly to raise ___ for things like growth or new products." Reveal note: "Money — that's the whole point of selling shares."
8. Prompt: "A company has 50 total shares. You own 5 of them. What percentage do you own?" Four answer options: (A) "10%" [correct], (B) "5%", (C) "50%", (D) "45%". Reveal shows the math: "5 ÷ 50 = 10%".

All other relevant info: Source — Aziz, Ch. 1 (adapted to be path-agnostic). No prerequisite level (first level of the entire app, first sub of Level 1). Deliberately the gentlest on-ramp in the whole app.
