Level: 9-1
Chapter: 1 — Market Basics
Path: Scalping (identical across all paths)
Category: New Theory
Subcategory: Market Structure
Learning Goal: User can distinguish a "bull market" (broadly rising) from a "bear market" (broadly falling) and understands these describe overall trends, not guarantees for every stock.
Purpose: These two terms show up constantly in financial news and conversation — knowing them lets you follow along instead of guessing at meaning.

Screens:
1. Title: "Zoom all the way out." Body: "Beyond single stocks and single days, the WHOLE market can trend up or down over months or years.".
2. Character 1/2.Text: "Bull market — prices broadly rising over time.".
3. Character 2/2.Text: "Bear market — prices broadly falling over time.".
4. Multiple choice. Prompt: "What does 'bear market' mean?"Four answer options: (A) "Prices are broadly falling over time" [correct], (B) "Prices are broadly rising over time", (C) "Only one company's stock is affected", (D) "The market is permanently closed". Reveal note: "Bear = broadly falling. Bull = broadly rising."
5. True/False. Statement: "In a bull market, every single stock goes up every single day."Two answer options "True" / "False" (correct). Reveal note: "Bull and bear describe the GENERAL trend — individual stocks can still move against it on any given day."
6. Scenario multiple choice. Prompt: "Over the past year, most major companies' stocks have been climbing steadily, even though a few individual stocks dropped along the way. What best describes this overall period?"Four answer options: (A) "A bull market" [correct], (B) "A bear market", (C) "A liquidity crisis", (D) "An index malfunction". Reveal note: "The overall climbing trend, despite some individual exceptions, is the hallmark of a bull market."
7. Matching exercise. Prompt: "Match each term to its meaning." Terms: "Bull Market", "Bear Market" matched to "Broadly rising over time" and "Broadly falling over time".

All other relevant info: Source — Murphy, Introduction (market trend terminology, simplified). Prerequisite: 8-2.
