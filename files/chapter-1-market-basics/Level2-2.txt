Learning Goal: User can name the main types of market participants (retail traders, market makers, institutional investors).
Purpose: Sudden, unexplained price jumps often come from these bigger players — recognizing who else is in the market helps you understand why prices sometimes move the way they do.

Reveal note: "Institutional investors often move far more money per trade than an individual retail trader."
7. Scenario multiple choice. Prompt: "A stock suddenly gets a wave of very large trades within seconds, all at once. Which type of participant is this most likely to be?"Four answer options: (A) "An institutional investor" [correct], (B) "A single retail trader with a small account", (C) "The company's own employees", (D) "A random computer glitch". Reveal note: "Sudden large trades are a common sign of institutional activity."
8. True/False. Statement: "A market maker's main job is to constantly buy and sell to help keep trading active."Two answer options "True" (correct) / "False". Reveal note: "That constant two-sided activity is exactly what a market maker provides."
