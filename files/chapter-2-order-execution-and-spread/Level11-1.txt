Learning Goal: User understands that transaction costs (fees, commissions) are a separate cost layer on top of Spread and slippage, and that even small per-trade fees add up at Scalping's high frequency.
Purpose: This is the last cost layer to know about — after this, you'll have the complete picture of what a trade really costs a Scalper.

3. Text: "A $1 fee feels tiny on one trade. Across 50 trades in a single day, that's $50 — before counting Spread or slippage at all.".

Reveal note: "It's the multiplication across many trades that makes this cost layer significant for Scalping."

Reveal note: "Once Spread, slippage, and fees are all stacked together, trade quality and selectivity become just as important as speed or frequency."
