6. Multiple choice. Prompt: "Based on the quote (Bid $45.20, Ask $45.24), what is the Spread?"Four answer options: (A) "$0.04" [correct], (B) "$45.20", (C) "$45.24", (D) "$90.44". Reveal shows the subtraction $45.24 − $45.20 = $0.04.
7. Scenario multiple choice. Prompt: "The quote shows a 'Last Trade' price of $45.22, between the Bid and Ask. What does this most likely represent?"Four answer options: (A) "The price at which the most recent trade actually executed" [correct], (B) "A typo in the platform", (C) "The average of all trades ever made", (D) "A prediction of tomorrow's price". Reveal note: "Last Trade shows what actually just happened — it often sits between Bid and Ask but isn't guaranteed to."
8. True/False. Statement: "On most platforms, you have to calculate the Spread yourself with no help from the interface."Two answer options "True" / "False" (correct). Reveal note: "Many platforms display the Spread automatically — but knowing how to verify it yourself is still valuable."
