What's most likely true about the Spread now?"Four answer options: (A) "It may have widened due to the sudden news-driven uncertainty" [correct], (B) "It's guaranteed to still be exactly $0.03", (C) "Spread size is now permanently fixed for this stock", (D) "News never affects Spread size".
